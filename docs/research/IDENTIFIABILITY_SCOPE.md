# What the comparison design audit can and cannot establish

## Exact algebra

A row of the specified two-indicator design has a 1 for its recorded player and a
1 for its observed context. A null vector v satisfies v_player + v_context = 0 on
every observed edge. Following a path forces all player entries in a connected
component to share one value a, and every context entry there to be −a. Different
components can choose independent values. Consequently nullity is exactly the
component count, and rank is observed vertices minus components. A player contrast
is orthogonal to this nullspace exactly when its two players share a component.

A spanning forest independently supplies V−C independent edge constraints. Tests
check all small binary designs against exact rational elimination, including cycles,
disconnections, duplicated rows and a bridge. No football response, coefficient,
prior, performance score or latent quantity is fitted. These are facts about the
specified observation design, not estimates of player characteristics.

Arbitrary component offsets can be fixed numerically through anchoring or priors.
That selects a coordinate convention/assumption; observations do not identify the
cross-component differences. Duplicating observations changes neither column rank
nor the missing bridges. Positive frequency weights likewise cannot add support.

## Counterarguments and scope

- **Exact cells may overcondition.** Discretizing location, period, score and native
  position is deliberately transparent but may fragment useful comparisons. Sparse
  exact cells do not prove that smooth spatial models or scientifically justified
  role groupings cannot work. Such models require defensible assumptions, authorized
  fitting and external tests. Do not loosen bins after looking at this audit solely
  to manufacture support.
- **The additive design is only one model class.** Allowing an arbitrary team-match
  effect is a conservative context specification. The theorem does not prove that
  all football models are unidentified or that abilities do not exist. A joint
  team-outcome design, standardized task or stronger substantive model is a different
  estimand/design and must be evaluated independently.
- **Opponent dependence differs from identification.** A schedule can connect all
  matches by shared opponents, while actor/own-context columns remain disconnected.
  Correlated outcomes across opponents do not insert observed player-team crossings
  into this design. Match/team/player dependence also prevents iid event inference.
- **Common support does not imply exchangeability.** Recorded passes are selected
  actions, not all alternatives available to a player. Conditioning on location or
  role can change the estimand or condition on consequences of earlier decisions.
  We make no causal adjustment claim. Native position evidence is a last observation,
  not certified continuous role; minutes/per90 and physical directions remain absent.
- **Connected contrasts need not be precise or useful.** Indirect graph paths can be
  fragile and additivity can fail. A pair sharing context in two matches and having
  five records per player is only a declared sparsity diagnostic. It does not certify
  sample adequacy, stable tendencies, construct validity or practical relevance.
- **Selection sensitivity is not a new outcome study.** Completion semantics define
  the stated subset; the audit is outcome-free in the sense of fitting no response.
  No previously consumed match is independent confirmation.

## Data requirements that would change the question

Request a longitudinal, provider-specific population only after original rights,
versions and exact files have been audited. Seek repeated players in meaningfully
crossed team/role contexts, independently assessed annotation quality, repeated
opportunities and untouched time/population blocks. A component-merging link count
is only a graph-connectivity lower bound; it is not a number of matches/transfers,
a power calculation or a sufficient scientific design. Transfers are selected,
not randomized interventions, so mobility alone does not identify causal ability.

One existing engineering assumption must be reviewed before such a future scope:
`fil.research.complete_corpus` currently rejects a player ID appearing for more than
one team. That is appropriate to the pinned national-team tournament identity audit,
but would reject legitimate club mobility. Do not weaken or silently reuse this gate.
A separately reviewed identity contract must validate per-match team membership,
registration/roster evidence, temporal identity continuity and provider semantics,
and distinguish legitimate team changes from ID collisions before acquisition.
The accepted policy, production allowlist and current source artifacts stay unchanged.

The next useful question is a bounded, context-conditioned behavior question with
independently annotated football relevance, not another universal player attribute.
A later blinded analyst comparison against a searchable event ledger tests utility;
repeated crossed observations test transport/stability. New prediction, hierarchical
or latent modeling requires specific source/use approval. Neither an agent system
nor a larger embedding fixes these evidence requirements by itself.
