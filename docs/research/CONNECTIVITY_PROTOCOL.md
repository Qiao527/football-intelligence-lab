Completed pass: original type Pass, absent `pass.outcome` key (explicit JSON null
is unknown), roster-valid distinct actor and recipient; match-play periods 1–4.
Keep raw pass evidence, including all exclusions. Native possession membership
must be contiguous, single-period and single-owner for sequence claims. No time
threshold or guessed physical boundary is used.

Chain: adjacent completed passes in that membership A→B and B→C. All intervening
records must be same-owner B Carry or successful Ball Receipt*; no other action,
opponent interruption, failed pass, ambiguous outcome or unassigned event may be
skipped. Receiving is recorded recipient evidence, not an observed physical touch.
Distinct A, B, C gives a non-return chain. Primary descriptor: non-return chains
whose first pass start and second pass endpoint occupy different 6×4 native cells.
Sensitivity: 3×2 cells. Coordinates are original StatsBomb 120×80, never flipped;
boundary values 120/80 belong to the last cell. Missing geometry stays null.
This is zone connection, not certified physical progression or attacking value.

Denominator: all geometrically observed eligible chains through B, including
returns. A conditional fraction describes this recorded sample, not ability or
exposure. Team completed-pass opportunities include the whole team-match, including
when a player may be absent; they are context, never a player availability denominator.
No minutes, per90 or normalized player comparisons using that denominator.

Baselines: completed/received volume; distinct outgoing/incoming degree; all
original player-actor record counts; native possession participation (actor or
completed-pass recipient); start-zone, period and strictly pre-pass score frequencies.
Record native position assignments with original Starting XI/Tactical Shift IDs,
roster metadata, substitutions and source order, without stable role inference.

Extract all rostered players, not a chosen star. Diagnostic eligibility: >=3
matches each with >=20 completed passes. Select six illustrative IDs by ascending
SHA256('statsbomb:'+player_id), independent of chain outcome and player fame.
For these players report every match, primary/secondary counts, between-match range
and absolute odd/even chronological split difference of conditional fractions,
with denominators. No significance test, ability score or ranking. Also summarize
all eligible players' within-player match ranges. No overlapping-action bootstrap.
Use team/player match-dependence components and counts, not an iid event sample.

Deterministic positive retrieval: earliest cross-zone non-return chain followed by
an owner Shot in the same verified membership. Counterexamples: earliest return,
earliest cross-zone non-return chain with no later recorded Shot (absence is not
proof of physical possession failure). Keep both original pass IDs, intervening
IDs, all later Shot IDs, and source manifest. Alternative paths are other actually
observed chains A→D→C within the same membership, never node deletion simulation.
Record descriptor/volume correlations as exploratory redundancy checks, including
zero/undefined cases. Correlation is neither incremental validity nor latent ability.
Use explicit within-context numerator/denominator counts; do not fit new models.
Scientific verdict must be INCONCLUSIVE until independent construct, repeatability,
context, incremental and analyst usefulness evidence passes a new frozen study.

All protected artifacts stay local/ignored. No new acquisition or rights expansion,
advanced model, runtime LLM, dashboard, merge, deployment or publication.
