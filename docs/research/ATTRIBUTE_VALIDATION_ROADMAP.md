# Attribute Validation Roadmap v1

Current verdict: **INCONCLUSIVE for latent player abilities**. Descriptive
connectivity extraction is available; risk-adjusted progression and continuity
remain research proposals. No numerical ability score or underrated-player claim
is warranted by the current tournament.

| Gate | Additional evidence / method | Failure condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rights | Exact original source versions, licences and local modeling permissions for each proposed fit | Ambiguous terms or unapproved use stops that fit/acquisition |
| Construct | Independent football experts annotate observable connections and counterexamples, blinded to FIL descriptors; lawful video only if authorized | Poor agreement or mismatch between operational chains and intended construct |
| Opportunities | Certified player availability and comparable roles, or a demonstrably adequate event-opportunity estimand | Team opportunities confounded with absence, selected observations or outcome missingness |
| Reliability | Repeated player-match-role support across teams/seasons, within-player contextual decomposition and prospective stability | Variation is predominantly context/noise; unstable thresholds or no identifiable player component |
| Context | Predeclared zone/role/team/opponent/score adjustments with common support | No overlap, disappearance after conditioning or excessive dependence on annotation density |
| Incremental validity | Frozen ledger/volume/location baselines and ablations for an external football task | No practically meaningful increment, poor calibration, or inconsistent counterexamples |
| Generalizability | Untouched cross-league, multi-season authorized provider-specific replication | Semantics/population invariance fails or only selected successful players replicate |
| Practical use | Blinded analyst crossover experiment vs searchable ledger | Unsupported claims rise, useful retrieval does not improve, or effect below predefined threshold |

## Replication protocol to freeze before acquisition

1. Complete a source-specific rights/identity audit; obtain an exact bounded owner
   approval. Provider datasets remain separate; do not pool event semantics silently.
2. Inventory coverage from metadata first: league, season, teams, players, role
   support, transfers, substitutions, event/spatial missingness and certified exposure
   availability. Select competitions by coverage/rights criteria, not observed effects.
3. Reserve an untouched league-season block and later-season block before payload
   access; record exact enumerations/hashes and personnel with prior exposure.
   Exclude all 64 current matches from independent confirmation. Distinguish unseen
   players/teams from repeated-player future-season transport; both are needed.
4. Freeze the operational definitions, zone-grid sensitivity, minimum support,
   opportunity denominator, exclusions/censoring, baselines, primary estimand and
   football outcome before opening held-out events. Power/sample planning uses
   development variance and team/player clustering, not number of overlapping actions.
   No fixed number of matches here is asserted sufficient without that calculation.
5. Authorize any specific hierarchical fitting separately. Assess team/player/role
   confounding and identifiability; use partial pooling only with adequate crossed
   support. Use complete-match temporal splits and held-team sensitivity; distinguish
   player stability from team effects. No xT/VAEP or new model-training rights here.
6. Retain all nulls, failures and frozen outputs. Independently reconcile raw source
   records, validate calibration where predictive targets apply, report uncertainty
   under match/team/player dependence and test measurement invariance. A tournament
   with one connected dependence component cannot certify independent replication.
7. Run the blinded utility task from the framework with independent relevance labels,
   concealed method identity, randomized/counterbalanced order, appropriate analyst
   and match clustering, predeclared errors and minimum worthwhile effect. Report
   disagreement and uncertainty, not only speed or attractive examples.

## Candidate-specific evidence

Attacking Connectivity needs independently annotated sequence relevance, common
role/zone support, repeatable behavior beyond pass volume and a useful retrieval
task. Counts and Pearson correlations from the current corpus are insufficient.
Risk-Adjusted Progression additionally needs validated failed-attempt denominators,
missing endpoints and authorized risk models; native dx is not physical intent or
defender pressure. Possession Continuity needs validated source closure/censoring
and annotation-density sensitivity; continuation is not causal retention skill.
Each construct can be rejected as not identifiable from ordinary event data.

## Current scale and network dependency

The corpus is one men's 2018 World Cup: 64 matches and 32 repeatedly interacting
national teams. Tournament advancement creates unequal match opportunity; many
players have sparse actor observations and each is nested in one team. Certified
minutes, stable roles, physical direction, continuous positions and off-ball actions
remain unavailable. This is useful methodological development evidence and cannot
establish a universal attribute or fair ranking across football populations.
