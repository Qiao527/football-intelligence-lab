# Independent evaluation and source decision

The frozen [recorded re-entry protocol](REENTRY_PROTOCOL.md) tests a team-possession
process, preserving the existing descriptor and every previous study. Its actual
numerical findings, full interpretation and analyst materials are protected local
artifacts, not public claims or human-validated discoveries.

## Which question is worth answering?

The principal unresolved question is whether source-supported sequence descriptions
help an analyst answer a meaningful buildup question more reliably than a searchable
event ledger with the same information. Before a descriptor becomes a proposed
football construct, establish what the records identify, what opportunity/context
can explain, whether the distinction is relevant to a human, and whether it recurs
outside the exposed tournament. None follows merely from implementing the query.

The present investigation deliberately chooses team processes rather than attributing
individual ability. It freezes a first-entry landmark, observation opportunity,
separate ordered/co-occurring patterns, censoring rules and conventional baselines.
There is no trained predictor, new player score or causal claim. Source geometry
does not establish pressure, intentional recycling or physical attacking direction.
Sparse conditional overlap cannot be repaired by citing the full corpus event count.
Zero-versus-zero cross-match fractions cannot establish repeatability of a behavior.

## Concrete independent task, ready for a feasibility pilot

`scripts/prepare_reentry_review.py` prepares an ignored local packet from the verified
study. It includes distinct-match tasks selected by identity hash in the frozen
motif/comparable-nonmotif/unresolved pools, complete source-only ledgers, identical
analyst ledgers, cards showing selected original records, blank judgment/response
sheets and counterbalanced planning slots. No participants, human labels, relevance
gold, timings or sample-size adequacy are fabricated. Slot IDs are design positions.

Independent source-only adjudicators see neither machine classifications nor cards.
They identify supporting/counterevidence IDs, ambiguities and relevance reasons;
disagreement is retained. Task analysts receive either a searchable complete ledger
or that same ledger plus a selected-source card. Information access is identical;
presentation/selection differs. An analyst sees no match/task twice. Independent
adjudication is not agreement with the algorithm, and interface blinding is incomplete.

The task asks analysts to locate evidence for/against a recorded retreat/re-entry
and judge whether the distinction helps compare buildup routes. It does not ask
them to certify intent, off-ball shape or player importance from event records.
Limit returned evidence to ten original action IDs and preserve all answer claims.
Useful pilot endpoints are defensible task completion, retrieval against independently
adjudicated IDs, unsupported claims, uncertainty, answer time and decision relevance.
An uncertain/empty relevance set has no invented recall or perfect score.

This is a balanced feasibility packet, not a representative prevalence sample or
independent out-of-corpus confirmation. Repeated cases/events are not independent
analyst observations. The eventual primary endpoint, worthwhile benefit, task wording,
participant qualification and clustered sample size must be ratified with independent
analysts before a separate confirmatory study. No outcome from this packet will be
relabeled as untouched validation after protocol tuning.

## Minimum new-source requirements and completed metadata assessment

Before new acquisition, require source-specific rights/version/file identities,
original action semantics and native coordinates, full matches with lineups and
per-match team/player continuity, source closure/missingness evidence, repeated
team/opponent contexts across at least two genuinely untouched temporal blocks,
and enough positive/common-support match clusters for the independently chosen
endpoint. Whole-match time blocks, repeated teams/players and annotation changes
must remain explicit. No fixed match count is automatically adequate, and no iid
event power calculation is justified. A bounded pilot estimates feasibility; it
does not constitute validation or authorize full expansion.

As authorized metadata-only planning, inspected the already acquired pinned
competition index and fetched only original match-index JSON for StatsBomb FA Women's
Super League seasons 2018/2019, 2019/2020 and 2020/2021. Paths are
`data/matches/37/4.json`, `data/matches/37/42.json`, `data/matches/37/90.json` at the
same pinned revision. Connector content was checked against Git blob identities and
recorded with local SHA-256 and retrieval receipts. No event, lineup or tracking
payload was requested. No production allowlist changed.

The exact local inventory retains metadata coverage, dates, team IDs and potential
calendar gaps. Metadata availability is not complete-season certification, matched
opportunity support, event quality, legal acquisition approval or statistical adequacy.
In particular, a missing fixture in an index cannot silently become an unplayed match.
Different seasons' coverage and source versions require audit, not silent pooling.
Women's club football is a distinct population from this men's tournament; a later
transport test must say so. Metadata-only exposure is logged; event/lineup payloads
were not opened by this task. Any future claim of untouched data needs an exposure
register, frozen eligibility and exact partition manifest before opening payloads.

The current finding does **not** justify requesting a broad new event corpus or
training scope. Resolve independent relevance and the access arrangement first.
If that pilot gives a worthwhile target, propose a bounded original-file quality
pilot and freeze the subsequent temporal study before inspecting new outcomes.
The current authorization covers no WSL event or lineup acquisition. No xT/VAEP,
predictive/hierarchical/latent fitting or model publication is proposed.

The original Wyscout/Figshare route remains an alternative, not an acquired corpus.
Runtime policy still lacks `api.figshare.com`; it was not retried or bypassed. If
the owner elects that route, the administrator action is metadata-only HTTPS GET
access to `api.figshare.com` for collection/article/version records. Actual original
file terms/versions still require review and separate archive authorization. A
paper's CC BY statement does not by itself finish that file-level review.

## Required decisions

No new decision is needed for the completed local descriptive study. To obtain
independent human evidence, the owner must establish a provider-compliant analyst
access route and make independent analyst participation available. That does not
authorize data redistribution. New event acquisition and model fitting should stay
on hold until a concrete, useful question and separately reviewed scope justify them.
The Figshare allowlist action is conditional on choosing that source route; it is
not a prerequisite for the current preparation or another repeated approval request.
