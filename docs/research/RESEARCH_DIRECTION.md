# Research direction: identify defensible comparisons before discovering attributes

The next milestone is an **outcome-free comparison design audit**. It examines
whether recorded opportunities support comparing players under explicit context
assumptions, before asking which metric or learner to fit. The immediate research
unit is a source-traceable recorded player action in context, not a latent ability
score. Team/process observations remain useful where individual separation is
unsupported. This challenges the earlier metric-first agenda without abandoning
its verified evidence layer.

## What earlier work establishes

Engineering establishes immutable provenance, reconciled source records, deterministic
pipelines, local auditability and verified code. It does not establish a player
construct. The frozen forecast concerns one specified recorded outcome; its result
is neither ability validation nor a causal account of football effectiveness.
Connectivity supplies real sequence evidence, but its opportunity/geometry sensitivity
and weak comparable-context support do not validate a distinct ability. Negative
findings are substantive measurement evidence and should change the agenda.
All current observations are exposed. Existing source timing, position/role,
independence and rights limitations remain; old artifacts and protocols are unchanged.
Protected empirical findings and the complete research-lead interpretation are local.

## Serious alternatives

| Direction | Scientific opportunity | Main requirement / limitation | Decision now |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| More event-derived attributes/networks | Cheap observable descriptions and retrieval | Can relabel volume/context; no independent construct labels | Pause new constructs; retain factual sequence tools |
| Action-value prediction | Useful forecast estimands and transparent benchmarks | Outcome/cutoff validity, rights, external validation; prediction is not ability | Preserve frozen results; no new fitting here |
| Hierarchical psychometrics / player-team decomposition | Repeatability and context separation | Crossed support, meaningful measurement model, fitting authorization | Audit design first |
| Causal player/team effects | Decision-relevant interventions | Defined intervention, exchangeability/positivity, selection/interaction design | No causal estimates from this tournament |
| Event representations / sequence models | Rich behavior/context representations | Training rights, data scale, transfer evaluation, construct interpretation | Study literature; do not train a new model |
| Tracking/spatial decision analysis | Observed options and off-ball context | New source rights, continuous spatial data, temporal calibration | Strategically promising, currently unavailable |
| Blinded analyst retrieval evaluation | Tests usefulness beyond a ledger | Independent analysts, relevance labels, randomized task and power design | Strong next evaluation partner; no fabricated human results |
| Longitudinal crossed corpus | Changes what can be learned about individuals | Exact rights, identity, role support, untouched population | Specify structural requirements before requesting data |
| PR integration / product optimization | Reliable delivery and review | Existing protection/acceptance decisions | Preserve guard; do not substitute governance for scientific work |

Highest immediate information value is the design audit: authorized, outcome-free,
reproducible and capable of showing whether more observations or more sophisticated
models would actually resolve the present ambiguity. No numeric ROI or universal
method ranking is claimed. The external-data direction must be revised if present
support turns out adequate under a useful estimand.

## Targeted literature review (not a systematic survey)

These are primary research sources. Abstracts/pages and the cited Kline et al.
sections were inspected; none of the empirical systems was independently replicated.
No code/data library licence is treated as provider-data or model-training permission.

- [Decroos et al., Actions Speak Louder Than Goals (2019), arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.07127v2)
  frames contextual on-ball action valuation. It motivates explicit forecasting
  targets, but FIL must not equate predicted action value with stable player ability.
- [Fernández & Bornn, SoccerMap (2020)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.10202)
  uses high-frequency spatial data to estimate pass probability surfaces. It shows
  why ordinary event records do not supply the same observed alternatives. This
  is a different data requirement, not a model to emulate with invented inputs.
- [Pappalardo et al., PlayeRank (2019), publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3343172)
  proposes multidimensional role-aware performance evaluation. Its abstract was
  verified in search; the institutional page returned 403, so no full-paper review
  is claimed. Role awareness is relevant; a ranking framework is not the present goal.
- [Kline, Saggio & Sølvsten, Leave-Out Estimation of Variance Components (2020)](https://eml.berkeley.edu/~pkline/papers/KSS2020.pdf)
  discusses limited-mobility bias and network bottlenecks in linked worker/firm
  designs (§§8–9, especially pp. 1882–1883). Our analogy is methodological: player
  and team separation depends on observed links. We implement no variance estimator.
- [Borsboom, Mellenbergh & van Heerden, The Concept of Validity (2004), author-institution abstract](https://cris.maastrichtuniversity.nl/en/publications/the-concept-of-validity/)
  argues for distinguishing a construct from correlations among measurements.
  This is one validity perspective, not an accepted causal proof for any football metric.
- [Hernán & Robins, Causal Inference: What If, author resource](https://miguelhernan.org/whatifbook)
  separates causal identification assumptions from estimation. Observed overlap is
  necessary for many comparisons but does not establish exchangeability or a
  meaningful football intervention. No causal method is fitted in this work.
- [Hong et al., EventGPT (arXiv v3, revised 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.17266v3)
  is a recent example of player-conditioned next-event representation learning.
  Its current primary abstract and author/version metadata were inspected; the
  earlier search title was stale. Full validation details require review before adoption. It is not evidence that learned player tokens identify abilities.

The original AKM/NBER page returned 403 and the plus-minus article page returned
429; neither was repeatedly retried, bypassed or presented as a verified full-text
source. Search hits alone do not validate a method. Public-literature access used
no protected provider records. Figshare metadata remains a separate administrator
access dependency; no Wyscout archive or new provider data was acquired.

## Commitments and next decision

The [frozen protocol](COMPARISON_DESIGN_PROTOCOL.md) specifies observations, algebra,
selection sensitivity, repeated support, missingness and independent verification.
The rank theorem is proved directly; it is not a result borrowed from a football
ranking paper. No response or player coefficient is fitted. A structural failure
under this design must not be generalized to all possible models or all football
abilities. Regularization can encode assumptions; it cannot create missing evidence.

After execution, use the observed support to define the smallest useful new research
population and an untouched validation task. Prefer context-conditioned behavior
and later repeated, crossed observations to unrestricted cross-team ability claims.
Before acquisition/fitting, review exact source files, rights, identifiers, repeated
role/opportunity coverage and any proposed model use. Do not request generic data
or expanded training rights simply because a sophisticated method is available.
