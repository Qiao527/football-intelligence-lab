# Hidden connector and bottleneck research design — pending validation

This is a proposed research protocol, not an implemented or validated discovery
engine. Current outputs are observed facts. No player rating or candidate label
is released from one match. Master Reference player/discovery scope and Handbook
research gates remain prerequisites, together with the repository research-evidence skill.

## Candidate generation and alternatives

Freeze definitions, population, analysis code and hypotheses before evaluation.
A potential connector could be screened for repeated involvement in recorded
owner-action sequences linking entries to later shots, reciprocal pass-to-shot
links and directed receipt/distribution patterns, conditional on observed action
exposure. A potential bottleneck could be screened for recurring observed sequence
terminations or low route diversity under comparable context. The screening rule
must specify event exclusions, thresholds, role cohort, missingness and direction
policy. Neither low volume nor a graph cut alone establishes a bottleneck.

Treat alternative explanations as required tests: assigned role, team circulation,
opponent pressing/context, score, period, red cards, restart mix, minutes,
provider recording coverage and opportunity. Event records cannot establish
unobserved support runs, availability, tactical intent or defensive coverage.
Static node deletion is descriptive sensitivity analysis, never causal removal.

## Data and exposure gates

Require verified multi-match event/lineup/substitution sources, stable IDs,
certified minute denominators, scoring and card chronology, native position/role
uncertainty, rights and missing-data coverage. Preserve candidate evidence IDs,
source/version, comparison population, role, state and exposure for every result.
Physical direction and tracking claims require independent source certification.
No estimated minutes or fabricated role labels may fill these gaps.

Proposed conservative eligibility floors for a future pilot are 10 complete
matches and 900 certified minutes per player, 200 eligible owner-action records,
and at least 30 eligible records in each reported comparison stratum. These are
planning floors, not proven power thresholds or production policy. Perform
simulation/power and stability analysis on an independently sourced development
corpus; choose and preregister final thresholds before holdout use. Sparse roles,
rare shot links and dependent team samples may require substantially more data.
Until then return insufficient evidence rather than extrapolate this fixture.

## Adjustment and independent validation

Separate observed fact, statistical inference and football interpretation.
Develop role/team/opponent and match-state adjustment using training matches
only. Use role metadata with uncertainty, never post-hoc labels chosen to elevate
a candidate. Compare both raw and adjusted estimates and show sample/support
counts. State overlap/positivity failures explicitly; do not compare unsupported
role/state strata. No causal language from observational adjustment.

Split whole matches chronologically, keeping repeated team/player dependence in
validation design; audit leakage and source-version differences. Freeze the
candidate screen before held-out matches. Seek independent football review of
original action evidence, including randomly chosen non-candidates and negative
examples. Test alternate possession definitions, restart exclusions, progression
thresholds, score strata and missingness assumptions. Require replication across
matches/opponents and preferably an independently verified provider corpus.

## Uncertainty, counterevidence and release gate

Use match-clustered resampling with team/player dependence where supported;
never bootstrap individual actions as independent trials. Report intervals,
rank/selection stability and sensitivity, not point estimates alone. Account for
all screened players/metrics with a preregistered multiplicity procedure (for
example Benjamini–Hochberg FDR 5% when its assumptions hold, otherwise a justified
dependent-testing method). Keep a held-out confirmation family separate from
exploratory screens. Statistical significance does not establish football value.

Explicit counterevidence includes disappearance under role/state adjustment,
poor holdout replication, recording artifacts, high selection instability,
alternative routes succeeding without the actor, and opportunities explained by
team dominance. Missing tracking precludes claims that a player was unavailable
or failed to move. Record tests that could overturn each candidate finding.

Release only `candidate_pending_review` or `inconclusive` until independent
replication, exposure certification, power/stability assessment, counterevidence
review and methodology/product approval pass. No hidden-connector, underrated or
low-contribution label is justified by the current France–Croatia fixture.
