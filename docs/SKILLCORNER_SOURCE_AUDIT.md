## Definitions and coverage (2026-11-07)

- The [pinned provider README](https://github.com/SkillCorner/opendata/blob/4340d274572876239c154c90bc507a9b3250a656/README.md) describes 10 Hz broadcast-video tracking, metre coordinates centred on the pitch, and `is_detected` as distinguishing detected from extrapolated player positions. Dynamic-event IDs are only unique within a match; phases are defined during ball-in-play. The provider reports approximately 97% player-identity accuracy, not perfect identity.
- SkillCorner describes its [passing-option output](https://landing.skillcorner.com/articles/metric-explainer-passing-options---engaging-the-first-line-of-pressure) as a receiver-model threshold, including options that were not passed to. It is a provider-derived label, not independent ground truth for our geometric screen. Detailed thresholds and independent accuracy for this open-data revision remain unverified.
- Across the 19 WARN bundles, QC counted 1,185,564 raw frames; 10,378,648 detected and 7,990,274 extrapolated **player-position records**. These are records, not unique players or complete-frame coverage. Ball flags were detected in 669,807 frames, extrapolated in 165,144, and absent/null in 350,613. These denominators include frames outside valid play and do not establish accuracy. The failed bundle is excluded.
- In match `1874553`, 46 of 6,031 every-tenth sampled frames passed the present conservative screen. This measures implementation eligibility, not the proportion of actual support runs; missingness, phase and possession coverage, boundaries and selection all matter. The screen applies 8–25 m ball-to-teammate distance and at least 2 m from each expected detected defender to that line segment. It is a static geometric candidate, not an assessed viable pass or movement.

## Local spatial replay

Run `.venv/bin/streamlit run scripts/skillcorner_replay.py --server.address 127.0.0.1` to review the local queue. The app verifies the local match and tracking file hashes against the review queue, requires the source audit to be WARN and the queue to be unreviewed, and shows source positions in a 10-second window at 2 Hz. Hollow markers are provider-extrapolated; missing or unknown observations are omitted. No interpolation, direction flip, ranking, video verification or external model call is performed. This is a local exploratory tool and is not the public synthetic demo.

The focus-player trail displays up to four seconds of detected positions and breaks at a missing, extrapolated, duplicate or nonconsecutive sampled observation. The dotted ball-to-player line is displayed only at the original screened frame. The ball distance and closest detected opponent distance use detected positions only; the closest detected opponent may not be the closest actual opponent. These measurements do not establish that a passing lane is usable, that a run was intentional, or that the player contributed to an outcome.

The two-second movement screen checks five consecutive 2 Hz sampled frames ending at the selected geometry frame. It requires one provider in-play phase, matching provider teammate possession and a different recorded carrier at each sample, and detected focus-player positions at all five samples. It marks straight-line displacement of at least 3 m as a candidate; the threshold is illustrative and uncalibrated. It does not establish what happened between samples, whether a player made a supporting run, or whether possession labels are accurate. On the ten preselected geometry frames, 18 player-frame pairs produced four candidates, one not meeting the rule because the focus was recorded as carrier, and 13 with insufficient data. These selected observations are not independent or representative, and this is not a measure of accuracy, recall, or player value.

Version `fil-tracking-movement-2` additionally checks the five original timestamps are valid and exactly 0.5 s apart; malformed phases, inconsistent clocks and shootout periods abstain. Outputs retain the player, team, period, sampled frame IDs, source timestamps and rule version. The 19 fictional scenarios in `tests/test_movement_screen_cases.py` check implementation behavior and missing-data handling, not tactical validity or real-world model performance. The time check parses the provider timestamp as a duration only and makes no assertion that its origin is half-relative or match-relative.
